# Fallback aircraft photo attribution

These photos are used as bundled fallbacks when a live JetPhotos/Planespotters
thumbnail is unavailable. They are cropped to 240x96 and dithered to the
panel's six-colour palette by `tools/make_fallback_photos.py`.
